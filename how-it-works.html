HOW IT WORKS

From your idea
to the final creation.

A simple process that turns your ideas, sketches and requirements into real 3D printed products.

01 💡

Share Your Idea

Share your idea, sketch, image, reference, measurements or simply explain what you want us to create.

02 ◈

3D Design

We convert your idea into a precise 3D model and prepare the design for high-quality and accurate printing.

03 ▣

Review & Quote

We share the design with you for review. Once everything is confirmed, we provide the final quote and estimated delivery time.

04 ▽

3D Printing

Once approved, we start printing your product using suitable materials, settings and 3D printing technology.

05 ⌕

Quality Check

Every finished product goes through a quality check. We clean, finish and inspect the product before packing.

06 ▣

Delivery

Your finished creation is carefully packed and delivered safely to your doorstep or made ready for pickup.
